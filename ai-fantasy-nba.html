Why injuries trip up AI assistants

Fantasy basketball decisions are nightly, and injury news moves faster than an AI's training data or a quick web search: a starter ruled out at shootaround, a return announced an hour before tip-off, a player who leaves in the second quarter. Naismith AI tracks the official NBA injury report, ESPN, and NBA insiders and beat writers, and hands your assistant the current status with its source and time.

What you can ask

  • “Here's my roster: … Anything I should know before tonight?”
  • “Is Jalen Brunson playing tonight?”
  • “How long is Chet Holmgren out, and when could he be back?”
  • “Who should I pick up with Stephen Curry out?”
  • “What injury news broke in the last few hours?”
  • “Who's coming back this week, and who loses minutes when he does?”

What the answers include

  • Status with its source
    Out, doubtful, questionable, probable or available, who reported it and when.
  • Return timelines
    The reported timeline, when it was reported, and the earliest return date it implies.
  • Who gains, with evidence
    Fantasy points per game each teammate should add, backed by the minutes they played with and without him in games he already missed.
  • Who loses value on a return
    The teammates whose minutes shrink when he comes back.
  • Tonight's slate
    Today's games with tip-off times and each team's injury news.

Numbers use points scoring. In a category league, read them as a guide to who gets more minutes.

Questions

Can ChatGPT or Claude tell me who's injured in the NBA tonight?

On their own, AI assistants answer from training data or a web search, which can be hours behind a player ruled out at shootaround. With the Naismith AI connector added, they read our live injury data instead: the official NBA injury report, ESPN, and NBA insiders and beat writers, usually updated within minutes of the news.

Can AI check my fantasy basketball roster for injuries?

Yes. Paste your roster (up to 30 names, misspellings are fine) and ask what you should know before tonight. You get each player's status, injury, return timeline, whether his team plays today and, for injured players, the teammate who gains the most.

Does Naismith AI connect to my ESPN, Yahoo or Sleeper league?

Not directly. It needs no account and never sees your league. Paste the players you care about into your assistant, or add a league connector alongside Naismith AI and your assistant can use both: your roster from one, the injury news from the other. Naismith AI doesn't do trades, rankings or draft advice: it covers player availability and the pickups an injury creates.

Who should I pick up when a player gets hurt?

Ask "Who should I pick up with [player] out?" Naismith AI ranks his teammates by the fantasy points per game each should add, with the evidence: minutes with and without him and what each teammate actually did in games he already missed. It also flags who is likely already rostered.

How fast is the injury news?

Usually within minutes. We check the official injury report, ESPN, NBA insiders and beat writers around the clock, and during games we check beat writers about once a minute for in-game exits and returns. Still, check before your lineup locks.
