Why injuries trip up AI assistants

There's a gap between a player getting hurt and working out who to pick up. An AI assistant's own knowledge is out of date, and a quick web search can miss news from the last hour.

Naismith AI gives it the current injury status and who gains, from the official NBA injury report and trusted NBA reporters.

Questions

Can ChatGPT or Claude tell me who's injured in the NBA tonight?

Not reliably on their own. Their built-in knowledge is out of date, and a quick web search can miss news from the last hour. With Naismith AI added, they get the current injury status.

Which AI assistants does it work with?

Claude and ChatGPT, both tested. Gemini works too (use Auto mode). There are steps for Grok, but we haven't tested it yet.

Can AI check my fantasy basketball roster for injuries?

Yes. Paste your roster (misspellings are fine) and ask what you should know before tonight. You get each player's status, when he's expected back and who gains if he sits.

Does Naismith AI connect to my ESPN, Yahoo or Sleeper league?

Not directly. It needs no account and never sees your league. Paste in the players you care about. It covers injuries and pickups, not trades, rankings or draft advice.

Who should I pick up when a player gets hurt?

Ask "Who should I pick up with [player] out?" You get his teammates ranked by the fantasy points per game each should add while he's out.

Where does the injury news come from?

The official NBA injury report and trusted NBA reporters. Still, check before your lineup locks.

Is it free?

Yes. Free, and no sign-in. It's also on X at @NaismithAIApp.
