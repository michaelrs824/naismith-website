Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

In short: no accounts, no cookies, no ad trackers. We count total page views.

Naismith AI posts NBA injury alerts and fantasy pickup suggestions on X (@NaismithAIApp) and at naismithai.com. This policy covers this website and its dashboard.

No accounts, nothing to sign up for

You don't sign up or log in. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or payment details.

Page-view analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which pages and referring sites people use. It doesn't use cookies, and it doesn't identify you or follow you across other sites. We only see totals.

The dashboard

When you open the dashboard or search for a player, your browser asks our server for that data. As with any website, our server and its host see your IP address. We use it only to answer the request and to limit abuse, never to identify you or build a profile.

Third parties

  • Vercel hosts this website and provides the analytics above.
  • Railway hosts the server the dashboard gets its data from.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces, so your browser requests it from Google.
  • X: if you follow or interact with @NaismithAIApp, X's own privacy policy applies.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or share data about visitors.

Children

Naismith AI isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
