  • "Who should I pick up with Stephen Curry out?"

What it can look up

  • A player's status and when he's expected back
  • Who gains when a player sits
  • Tonight's games and each team's injury news
  • The best pickups for 10-team, 12-team or deep leagues
  • The latest injury news from the last few hours
How the numbers work
  • Numbers use points scoring: points 1, rebounds 1.2, assists 1.5, steals 3, blocks 3, threes 0.5, turnovers −1.
  • When a player has missed games before, they lean on what each teammate actually did in those games.
  • Otherwise they're a conservative model projection.
  • In a category league, read them as a guide to who gets more minutes.
  • Statuses come from the official NBA injury report and trusted NBA reporters.
  • Check before lineups lock.
For developers
