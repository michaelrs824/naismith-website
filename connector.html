Naismith AI in your AI assistant

Add Naismith AI to Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, then ask about NBA injuries in plain words. You get our live status, return timelines and who to pick up, the same data behind our alerts on X. Free, read-only, no sign-in.

Add to ChatGPT

Connector address

https://www.naismithai.com/mcp/v1

Authentication: none. Transport: Streamable HTTP.

Add it

  • Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Name it Naismith AI and paste the address.
  • ChatGPT: open Naismith AI in ChatGPT's plugin directory and press Install plugin.
  • Gemini: Apps → Connectors → + → Connect to an MCP server. Paste the address, press Next, accept and Connect. In a chat, type @ and pick Naismith AI. Works best in Auto mode.
  • Grok (paid plans, not yet tested by us): grok.com → Connectors → New connector → Custom. Choose Streamable HTTP, paste the address and set Authentication to None.

Menus change often; if a step looks different, look for "connectors", "apps" or "MCP" in your assistant's settings. Assistants that support prompts also get three ready-made ones: check my roster, who to start tonight, and best pickups.

What to ask

  • "Here's my roster: … Anything I should know before tonight?"
  • "Who should I pick up with Stephen Curry out?"
  • "Best adds right now for a 12-team league?"
  • "What injury news broke in the last few hours?"
  • "Who's out tonight for the Lakers and Celtics?"
  • "Is Jalen Brunson playing tonight?"

AI for fantasy NBA

What it can look up

  • Check players: status (out, doubtful, questionable, probable, available), the injury, return timeline and earliest return date, whether his team plays today and how many games it has left this week, and for injured players the teammates who gain the most. Up to 30 names at once; partial or misspelled names work.
  • Who gains when a player sits: projected fantasy points per game each teammate should add, with the evidence: minutes with and without him and what each teammate actually did in the games he missed.
  • Tonight: today's games with tip-off times (ET) and each team's injury news, or just the games your players are in.
  • Best pickups: the top adds across every current injury, for 10-team, 12-team or deep leagues, with the share of ESPN leagues that already roster each one.
  • Latest news: status changes from the last few hours (or since your last check), with the insider who reported it.

Numbers use points scoring (points 1, rebounds 1.2, assists 1.5, steals 3, blocks 3, threes 0.5, turnovers −1). When a player has missed games before, they lean on what each teammate actually did in those games; otherwise they're a conservative model projection. In a category league, read them as a guide to who gets more minutes. Statuses come from the official NBA injury report, ESPN, and NBA insiders and beat writers, usually within minutes. Check before lineups lock.
