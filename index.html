How it works

Sources. We check Shams Charania and Underdog NBA about every 90 seconds, other main insiders every 10 minutes and beat writers about every 50. We also read the official NBA injury report, ESPN, RotoWire, CBS Sports and Bluesky.

Alerts. We post when a player is ruled out, listed doubtful or questionable, or gets a new timeline. When a player leaves a game, we post that too, and if he comes back within about four hours, we reply under the post.

Accuracy. An AI model writes the one-line summary. Code then checks it against the original report and rejects any team, day, body part or timeline the report doesn't contain. It still gets things wrong sometimes.

Scoring. Points 1, rebounds 1.2, assists 1.5, steals 3, blocks 3, threes 0.5, turnovers -1.
